## Prerequisites

Before diving in, ensure you have:

- A valid email address
- A payment method (credit card or PayPal)
- Access to your email for verification

<Callout kind="info">
  AppSumo offers lifetime deals on productivity tools, so no recurring subscriptions required.
</Callout>

## Create Your Account

Sign up takes under 2 minutes. Choose your preferred method.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Email Signup" icon="mail">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Visit Signup Page" icon="external-link">
        Go to <a href="https://appsumo.com/signup">appsumo.com/signup</a>.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter Details">
        Provide your email, create a password, and accept terms.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Verify Email">
        Check your inbox for the verification link and click it.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Google Signup" icon="apple">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Click Google Button">
        On the signup page, select "Continue with Google".
      </Step>
      <Step title="Authorize">
        Grant AppSumo access to your Google account.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Use a business email for order management notifications.
</Callout>

## Complete Your First Purchase

Ready to buy? Follow these steps for a smooth checkout.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Deal" icon="shopping-cart">
    Click a deal card, then choose your tier (e.g., 1-user or unlimited).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to Cart">
    Hit "Get Deal" and review in cart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Checkout">
    Enter payment details and complete purchase. You'll receive instant access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access Software">
    Check your email for login credentials or dashboard links.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage Your Profile and Orders

Access your dashboard at <a href="https://appsumo.com/dashboard">appsumo.com/dashboard</a>.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Profile" icon="user">
    Update email, password, or billing info under Account Settings.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Orders" icon="package">
    View purchases, download licenses, and track renewals.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Expandable title="Troubleshooting Common Issues" default-open="false">
  - Forgot password? Use the reset link on login.
  - Order not received? Check spam folder or contact support@appsumo.com.
</Expandable>
