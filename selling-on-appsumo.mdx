## Overview

AppSumo connects your software products with over 1 million entrepreneurs looking for lifetime deals on productivity, marketing, and content tools. As a vendor, you gain access to a massive audience without monthly subscription hassles for buyers. This guide walks you through account setup, listing creation, pricing, and promotion to maximize sales.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Prepare your product demo, testimonials, and sales page before starting. AppSumo prioritizes high-quality, vetted software.
</Callout>

## Set Up Your Vendor Account

Getting approved as a vendor requires a quick application process. AppSumo reviews submissions to ensure product-market fit.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up" icon="user-plus">
    Visit `https://appsumo.com/vendors` and create an account using your business email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Application" icon="upload">
    Fill out the vendor form with your product details, target audience, and revenue projections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Await Approval" icon="clock">
    Expect a response within 3-5 business days. Provide additional info if requested.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access Dashboard" icon="dashboard">
    Once approved, log in to your vendor dashboard at `https://vendors.appsumo.com`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create Your Product Listing

Craft compelling listings that highlight value. Use high-quality screenshots and videos to showcase features.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare Assets" icon="image">
    Gather 5-10 screenshots, a 2-minute demo video, and customer testimonials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build Listing" icon="edit-3">
    In the dashboard, select "New Deal" and enter product name, description, and features.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Deal Terms" icon="settings">
    Define tiers, pricing, and limitations like user seats or update periods.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for Review" icon="check-circle">
    Preview and submit. AppSumo team reviews for compliance within 48 hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

```json
{
  "product": {
    "name": "TaskMaster Pro",
    "description": "Ultimate task automation for teams",
    "features": ["Unlimited workflows", "Integrations with 50+ apps"]
  },
  "tiers": [
    {
      "name": "Starter",
      "price": 49,
      "seats": 5,
      "updates": "1 year"
    }
  ]
}
```

## Pricing Strategies and Deal Structures

Choose structures that align with your business model. Lifetime deals drive volume sales.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Lifetime Deals" icon="dollar-sign">
    Offer one-time payments for perpetual access. Popular for tools under `{< $100}` per tier.
    
    <Callout kind="success">
      Lifetime deals convert 3x higher on AppSumo.
    </Callout>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Tiered Pricing" icon="layers">
    Create 3-5 tiers based on seats or features. Example: `{49, 99, 199}`.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Limited Quantities" icon="zap">
    Cap sales at 500-2000 units to create urgency and FOMO.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
