## List Your Product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up as a Partner">
    Go to https://partners.appsumo.com and create an account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Your Product">
    Fill in your product info, upload assets, and add pricing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and Launch">
    Review dashboard for completeness. Hit "Submit for Approval".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor Status">
    Once approved, your listing goes live. Track performance in your partner dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>
