---
title: How do I redeem a deal on AppSumo?
description: Steps to redeem your purchased deal code.
---

## Overview

After purchasing, you'll receive a unique redemption code.

## Steps

1. Go to your AppSumo account dashboard
2. Click on "Products" to see your purchases
3. Click "Redeem" next to the product
4. Copy your unique code
5. Follow the product's redemption link
6. Paste your code during sign-up or checkout

<Callout kind="info">
Redemption instructions are found on your purchase page.
</Callout>
